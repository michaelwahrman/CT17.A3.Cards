How to play
Klondike
  • Build the four foundations (top right) up by suit from Ace to King.
  • In the seven columns, build down in alternating colours. Only a King may go into an empty column.
  • Click a face-up card (or the top of a run) and it makes its best move: up to a foundation if it can, otherwise onto a column. Drag to choose the place yourself.
  • Next Card, Space, or a click on the stock turns 1 or 3 cards (Draw 1 / 3).
  • Deck uses is how many times you may go through the stock (default 3). When the stock is empty, Next turns the deck over.
  • When every card is face up and the stock is empty, the game finishes itself.
FreeCell
  • All 52 cards are dealt face up into eight columns. Build the four foundations (top right) up by suit from Ace to King.
  • The four free cells (top left) each hold one card. In the columns, build down in alternating colours; any card may go into an empty column.
  • You may move a run of cards at once if there is room to do it one card at a time: up to (free cells + 1) × 2empty columns.
  • Click a card and it makes its best move: foundation, else onto a column, else a free cell, else an empty column. Drag to choose yourself.
  • Auto-play sends cards that are no longer needed up to the foundations by themselves (one Undo takes back your move and them).
  • Game # — the classic Microsoft deals 1–32000 (same layouts as Windows FreeCell). Type a number and press Enter.
  • Klondike / FreeCell at the top switches game (G). ● Record (V) records a movie with sound at the size of the table on your screen; press again to stop — the MP4 downloads.
  • Keys: Space/N next · U or Ctrl+Z undo · D deal · G switch game · V record · H help
